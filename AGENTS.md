# Bilingual content

- Keep the Ukrainian homepage (`index.md`, published at `/`) and the English page (`en/index.md`, published at `/en/`) equivalent in information.
- Whenever information is added, updated, or removed, make the corresponding change in both pages in the same task, using natural Ukrainian and English respectively. Do not leave either language version outdated or partially translated.
- Apply the same rule to shared interface text and metadata: update both `_data/i18n/uk.yml` and `_data/i18n/en.yml` whenever their content changes.
- Preserve matching explicit section IDs across both pages so navigation links continue to work.
